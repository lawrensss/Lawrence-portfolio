### 🌍 [Global Climate Risk & Economic Impact Intelligence System](https://github.com/lawrensss/climate-risk-intelligence)

> *End-to-end data pipeline connecting NASA climate science with World Bank economics*

- Built a **fully automated Python pipeline** that ingests, cleans, and loads real data from NASA GISTEMP and World Bank APIs into a structured SQLite star schema database
- Engineered key features including **10-year rolling temperature averages**, anomaly tier classification, and GDP per capita derivation across 15 global regions
- Discovered that **2024 was the hottest year on record at 1.29°C** above baseline — and that all 5 years above the 1.0°C danger threshold have occurred since 2016
- Revealed that the **most agriculture-dependent regions carry the lowest GDP** — quantifying economic vulnerability to climate shocks
- Built a **3-page interactive Power BI dashboard** with cross-filtering slicers connecting temperature trends, economic indicators, and regional risk profiles
- Produced a **Google Colab analysis notebook** with 3 publication-quality charts exploring the climate-economy relationship from 1970 to 2025
- Designed with **production engineering principles**: raw data never modified, parquet intermediate storage, modular scripts, full logging, and a config-driven architecture

`Python` `SQLite` `pandas` `Power BI` `Google Colab` `REST APIs` `Parquet` `Star Schema`
